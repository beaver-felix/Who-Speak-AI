\subsection{Introduction to Who-Speak-AI}
\emph{Who-Speak-AI} is a voice-driven virtual assistant that integrates biometric speaker recognition into a modular conversational pipeline. By coupling speech processing with our fine-tuned RawNet3 speaker embedding model, the assistant provides seamless voice interaction while enforcing biometric verification for sensitive operations and personalizing responses based on speaker identity.
