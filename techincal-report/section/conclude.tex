This report benchmarked ECAPA-TDNN and RawNet3 across multilingual (TidyVoice) and Vietnamese multi-dialect (ViMD) conditions. While ECAPA-TDNN demonstrated stronger multilingual generalization (3.912\% vs.\ 5.875\% EER on TidyVoice), RawNet3 excelled on regional Vietnamese speech (3.071\% vs.\ 3.804\% EER and 0.2262 vs.\ 0.4516 minDCF on ViMD), accompanied by 21.6\% fewer parameters and 26.5\% lower GPU latency. Consequently, \textbf{RawNet3 fine-tuned on ViMD} is selected as the biometric verification engine for the \emph{Who-Speak-AI} virtual assistant.
